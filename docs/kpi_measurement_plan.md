# KPI Measurement Plan

## Purpose

This document defines how the project will measure new-player conversion and
retention.

## Player journey

1. A potential player signs up.
2. The player makes a first purchase.
3. The player attends a first organized-play event.
4. The player returns for another event.
5. The player continues participating over time.

## Purchase conversion rate

### Business question

What percentage of new signups purchase a Riftbound product within 30 days?

### Formula

Purchase conversion rate =
Players purchasing within 30 days / Total new signups

### Numerator

Number of players who make a first purchase within 30 days of signing up.

### Denominator

Total number of new players who signed up.

### Required data

- Player ID
- Signup date
- First-purchase date
- Product purchased

## First-event conversion rate

### Business question

What percentage of purchasers attend an organized-play event within 45 days
of their first purchase?

### Formula

First-event conversion rate =
Purchasers attending within 45 days / Total purchasers

### Numerator

Number of purchasers who attend their first event within 45 days.

### Denominator

Total players who made a first purchase.

### Required data

- Player ID
- First-purchase date
- Event date
- Attendance status

## Player retention

### Business question

What percentage of players attend another event after their first event?

### Formula

Retention rate =
Players attending another event within the measurement window /
Players attending a first event

### Measurement windows

- 30-day retention
- 60-day retention
- 90-day retention

### Required data

- Player ID
- First-event date
- Subsequent event dates
- Attendance status

## Important limitations

Event attendance does not capture every form of Riftbound engagement. A player
may remain active by collecting cards, playing at home, watching content, or
participating in an online community without attending another official event.
